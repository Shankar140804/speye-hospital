FAQ

Common Questions

Can't find your answer? Call us on +91 79474 33442.

Is cataract surgery painful?

No. It is done under eye drops or local anaesthesia, takes about 15–20 minutes and most patients go home the same day.

Am I suitable for LASIK?

Most adults above 18 with a stable glass power are suitable. A detailed pre-LASIK check-up confirms the best procedure for you.

How often should I get my eyes checked?

Once a year for adults, and every 6 months for diabetics, glaucoma patients and children using glasses.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we offer cashless treatment with most leading insurance companies and TPAs.
