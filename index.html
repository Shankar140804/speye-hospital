About Us

Trusted Eye Care for Every Generation

S.P.Eye Hospital is a dedicated super-speciality eye care centre offering complete diagnosis, medical and surgical treatment for all eye conditions under one roof. Our team of experienced ophthalmologists, optometrists and nurses combine compassion with the latest technology to protect and restore your vision.

Our Treatments

Complete Eye Care Services

From routine eye check-ups to complex surgeries, we offer comprehensive care for all your vision needs.

Cataract Surgery

Painless phaco & femto laser cataract surgery with premium foldable lenses.

LASIK & Refractive

Get rid of glasses with LASIK, SMILE, PRK and ICL procedures.

Retina Care

Diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, retinal detachment & injections.

Glaucoma

Early detection, pressure monitoring, laser and surgical glaucoma management.

Cornea & Keratoconus

Corneal transplant, C3R cross-linking and treatment for eye infections.

Pediatric & Squint

Child eye exams, lazy eye, squint correction and myopia control.

Oculoplasty

Eyelid surgery, droopy lids (ptosis), tear duct and orbit procedures.

Eye Check-up & Optical

Comprehensive eye exams, contact lenses and in-house optical store.
