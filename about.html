About Us

Trusted Eye Care for Every Generation

S.P.Eye Hospital is a dedicated super-speciality eye care centre offering complete diagnosis, medical and surgical treatment for all eye conditions under one roof. Our team of experienced ophthalmologists, optometrists and nurses combine compassion with the latest technology to protect and restore your vision.

Founded with a simple belief – that everyone deserves clear, healthy vision – we have grown into one of the region's most trusted eye hospitals, treating patients of every age with honesty, skill and care.

  • Bladeless & micro-incision cataract surgery
  • Modern LASIK / SMILE vision correction
  • Fully equipped modular operation theatres
  • Cashless insurance & easy payment options
